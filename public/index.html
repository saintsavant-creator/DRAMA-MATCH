your Asian drama questions, answered

What K-drama should I watch if I like Stranger Things?

If you love Stranger Things, try Signal (2016) — a supernatural crime thriller with a time-bending plot that connects past and present — or Sweet Home (2020), a monster-horror survival drama with the same ensemble cast energy. Both score 60%+ matches on DramaMatch. See all K-drama matches for Stranger Things →

What is the Korean version of Breaking Bad?

The closest Korean equivalent to Breaking Bad is Stranger (Forest of Secrets, 2017) — a dark crime drama with morally complex characters and deep institutional corruption. Connection (2024) and Bad Guys also share the same crime-thriller DNA. See all K-drama matches for Breaking Bad →

What K-drama is similar to Game of Thrones?

Six Flying Dragons (2015–2016) is the closest K-drama to Game of Thrones — a sweeping historical epic with political intrigue, shifting alliances, and morally complex power struggles. Kingdom (2019) delivers the same epic scale with a zombie-meets-Joseon-dynasty thriller. See all matches for Game of Thrones →

Best K-dramas for beginners?

The best entry-point K-dramas are: Crash Landing on You (romance, Netflix), Goblin (fantasy romance, Netflix/Viki), Vincenzo (crime comedy, Netflix), My Mister (drama, Netflix), and Reply 1988 (slice-of-life, Netflix). Not sure where to start? Search any Western show you love on DramaMatch and we'll find your perfect first K-drama.

How do I find K-dramas, C-dramas & J-dramas similar to Western shows?

DramaMatch uses a 6-dimensional algorithm comparing genres, themes, tropes, tone, setting, and character types to find K-dramas, C-dramas, and J-dramas that match any Western show. Search your show above and get ranked recommendations instantly — with match percentage and reasoning for each pick. Use the filter tabs to browse by drama type.

Where can I watch K-dramas, C-dramas & J-dramas for free?

Viki offers a large K-drama and C-drama library free with ads — it's the best free option. Tubi has a selection of older titles for free as well. Netflix, Amazon Prime, Disney+, and Hulu require subscriptions but carry the most titles including J-dramas. DramaMatch shows which platforms carry each drama on every recommendation page.

What are the highest-rated K-dramas of all time?

Critically acclaimed K-dramas include My Mister, Signal, Reply 1988, Mr. Sunshine, and Misaeng. For romance fans, Crash Landing on You and Goblin consistently rank at the top. DramaMatch helps you find which of these best matches your personal taste.

What K-drama is like The Office or Parks and Recreation?

For workplace comedy fans, try Chief Kim (2017) — a comedy about a con artist turned corporate hero who accidentally becomes a workplace champion — or Welcome to Waikiki (2018) for ensemble slapstick humor in a guesthouse setting.
