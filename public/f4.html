F4 Member · 花澤類

Darren Chen

陳大天 Hua Ze Lei b. Nov 26, 1993

Taiwanese actor and model who became a cultural phenomenon practically overnight. Darren entered acting through the modeling world, and that background shows — he carries every scene with a quiet, photogenic stillness that's genuinely rare. His portrayal of Hua Ze Lei, the silent, intellectually intense member of the F4, drew comparisons to the original 2001 version and held up. He plays introspective the way some actors play loud: with full commitment.

Post-Meteor Garden he pivoted toward more complex roles, earning a reputation for picking projects that challenge him rather than ones that coast on his looks. He speaks Mandarin and Taiwanese Hokkien, and has been quietly candid in interviews about being an introvert — which tracks completely with his Hua Ze Lei energy.

Fun Facts
  • 🎭His very first major acting role was the 2016 hit film Our Times — he landed Meteor Garden just two years later.
  • 🤫Described by co-stars as genuinely quiet and introverted on set — he barely had to act for Hua Ze Lei's quieter scenes.
  • 📚Has said in interviews that he reads extensively between projects — philosophy and fiction, which feeds into the thoughtful quality he brings to roles.
  • 🎸Like his character, he has a connection to music — he's an amateur guitar player who occasionally posts covers online.
  • 🌟His international fanbase exploded post-Meteor Garden, particularly in Southeast Asia — a region Taiwanese actors hadn't consistently reached since the original F4 era.

CW
F4 Member · 西門

Caesar Wu

吳希澤 Ximen b. Nov 16, 1994

Caesar walked onto the Meteor Garden set with limited acting experience and walked off with a dedicated fanbase who'd follow him anywhere. That's partially charm — Caesar is genuinely warm in a way that reads as effortless on screen — and partially his work ethic. By his own account, he put everything into learning the craft on the job, and it shows in the progression of his work.

His character Ximen is the elegant, slightly mysterious one: old money polish, romantic damage beneath the surface. Caesar leaned into the contradictions — the charm that masks depth, the playfulness that suddenly turns sincere. He's gone on to lead several well-received C-dramas on his own, showing range that goes beyond heartthrob territory.

Fun Facts
  • 💪He openly talks about the pressure he felt entering Meteor Garden with minimal acting experience — said it motivated him to be on set early every day to rehearse.
  • 😂Known as the funniest on set during the Meteor Garden shoot — the blooper reels are largely his work.
  • 🎵Unlike his violin-playing character Ximen, Caesar's actual hobby is fitness and boxing — a contrast the internet found deeply funny.
  • 📈His Weibo following went from negligible to several million within weeks of Meteor Garden's premiere — one of the sharpest inflection points of any cast member.
  • 🏆Won Best New Actor awards at multiple Chinese drama award ceremonies following the show's success in 2018.

CL
F4 Member · 美作

Connor Leong

梁靖康 · also known as Leon Leong Meizuo b. Feb 18, 1996

The youngest F4 member, Connor (梁靖康, also known as Leon Leong) plays Meizuo — the carefree, musical, secretly-softhearted one. Which, it turns out, is not a huge stretch. Connor is genuinely musical in real life and carries the same easy, unguarded warmth his character does. What he brings to the role that could have been pure comic relief is a grounded sincerity that makes Meizuo's arc land.

Post-Meteor Garden he's been one of the busiest of the four — leading period dramas, contemporary romance, and even genre-blending projects that suggest he actively resists being boxed in. He's earned a reputation as an actor's actor among C-drama insiders: someone other creators want to cast because he'll give you something you didn't script.

Fun Facts
  • 🎹Actually plays piano and guitar — his Meizuo character's love of music wasn't a stretch. He genuinely serenades people in real life, apparently.
  • 😎Widely cited by the other three F4 members as the one who kept morale up during the long shoot — the unofficial emotional support of the cast.
  • 📚Graduated from a performing arts academy before Meteor Garden — more formal training than most of his co-stars, which shows in his comedic timing.
  • 🌸His post-Meteor Garden drama Ancient Love Poetry became one of the most-watched C-dramas of 2021, cementing him as a lead actor in his own right.
  • 🎂The youngest of the four F4 actors — was 22 years old when Meteor Garden premiered in 2018.

The 2018 remake of Meteor Garden (流星花园) is a Chinese adaptation of the iconic Taiwanese drama and manga Hana Yori Dango. Where the 2001 original made Jerry Yan, Vic Chou, Ken Zhu, and Vanness Wu into pan-Asian celebrities, the 2018 version took four relatively unknown young actors and did it again — in the streaming era, with a global audience.

The show aired on Hunan TV and Mango TV in 2018, accumulating billions of views across platforms in its first weeks. It was simultaneously one of the most-watched C-dramas of the year and a genuine cultural flashpoint: people who hadn't touched Chinese dramas in years tuned in. People who'd never watched C-dramas started.

What made the 2018 F4 work was chemistry that felt genuinely un-manufactured. The four had little in common going in — different training, different backgrounds, different temperaments — and somehow became exactly the kind of tight-knit unit the script required. The behind-the-scenes footage from the shoot tells that story better than any press release.
